About 8KStream

8KStream is an independent website and customer-support brand that helps visitors compare IPTV subscriptions, simultaneous-connection options and reseller panel packages. 8KStream is not the name of an IPTV server.

Our approach

We present plan duration, connection limits, device compatibility and reseller credit information in a clear format. Availability, channel lineups and activation details are confirmed with customers before purchase.
