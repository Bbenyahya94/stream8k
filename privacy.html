Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you browse 8KStream or contact the support team.

Information you provide

When you contact 8KStream, you may provide contact details, device information, selected products and order-related information needed to answer your request or provide support.

Technical and analytics information

Hosting and security providers may process standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and timestamps. This website uses Google Analytics with measurement ID G-K24ZKWH53Y to understand aggregate website usage and improve navigation and performance.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, prevent abuse, diagnose errors and improve website reliability. 8KStream does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Telegram, email, Google Analytics, hosting and security providers process information under their own policies when their services are used. Only send information needed for your request.

Retention and choices

Contact information is retained only as reasonably needed for support, service, legal or security purposes. Browser settings and privacy tools can be used to limit cookies or analytics storage.
