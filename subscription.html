PLAN GUIDE

How to choose the right IPTV subscription

Check your devices, connection needs and internet quality before placing an order.

Choose your duration

Plans are available for one, three, six or twelve months. Longer packages offer better overall value where shown.

Select connections

One connection means one simultaneous stream. Eligible products let you select two to five concurrent streams with automatic package discounts.

Confirm compatibility

Ask support to confirm compatibility with your Smart TV, Firestick, Android device, Apple device, computer or TV box.
