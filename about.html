ABOUT 8KSTREAM

Clear comparisons and practical IPTV support

8KStream helps viewers and resellers compare plans, connection limits, pricing and setup requirements before they order.

Our purpose

We organize IPTV subscription durations, simultaneous-connection options and reseller credit packages in a clear, consistent format.

Our approach

Availability, channel lineups, device compatibility and activation details should be confirmed with customers before purchase.

Our identity

8KStream is the website and customer-support brand. It is not the name of an IPTV server or a claim of guaranteed 8K playback.

What customers can compare

Visitors can review one, three, six and twelve-month subscription options, eligible multi-connection discounts and reseller starter packages from the listed service brands.

Responsible service information

Streaming performance depends on the selected provider, device capability, internet connection, location and third-party infrastructure. Customers and resellers are responsible for lawful use under applicable local rules.

Contact and review

Questions can be sent to support@8kstream.net or through the contact page.
